FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our entrepreneurship development programs.

What are the eligibility requirements for the PGP in Entrepreneurship & Family Business program?

Graduates in any discipline are eligible to apply. The program particularly welcomes entrepreneurs with some experience, current business owners, family business successors, and working professionals who want to transition to entrepreneurship. While work experience is preferable, it's not mandatory.

How is the program structured and what is the duration?

The program is an 11-month course structured into three terms plus an internship. Term 1 (Sept-Nov) focuses on Business Foundations at IILM, Term 2 (Nov-Jan) covers Advanced Business & Growth Strategies, followed by a 100-hour internship (Feb-March), and Term 3 (April-June) includes specialization courses and a capstone project at EDII Ahmedabad.

What is the fee structure and are scholarships available?

The program fee is INR 5.5 Lakhs + GST. Several scholarship options are available including merit-based scholarships, a 10% Tuition Fee Waiver scheme, and special scholarships for female participants to ensure financial constraints don't prevent deserving candidates from joining.

What makes this program unique compared to other entrepreneurship courses?

The program offers dual-campus experience (Delhi and Ahmedabad), focuses on both entrepreneurship and family business management, provides five specialization pathways, includes 100+ hours of internship experience, offers international immersion opportunities in China and Spain, and provides potential access to seed funding for startups.

What are the career outcomes after completing this program?

Graduates can start and scale their own ventures, expand and innovate their family businesses, lead high-growth startups and corporations, or work as business consultants and advisors. The program also provides post-program support including business mentorship, incubation support, access to funding networks, and continued alumni engagement.
